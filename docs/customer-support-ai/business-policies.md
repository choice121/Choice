# Choice Properties — Business Policies & Standards

This document serves as the single source of truth for business policies used by the AI Rental Inquiry Assistant and customer operations.

## 1. Core Financial Policies
- **Application Fee**: Exactly **$50** per adult applicant / property.
  - Never state "$0 fee", "free application", or "fee is waived".
  - If the application fee policy is modified centrally, this document and `policies.json` are updated simultaneously.
- **Security Deposit**: Exactly **1x monthly rent** in structured database records.
  - **Zero Mentions Policy**: Deposit amounts, terms, and quotes must NEVER be volunteered or quoted in initial customer inquiry messages or listing descriptions.
  - If a customer explicitly asks about the security deposit amount, state: *"The standard security deposit is equal to one month's rent upon approved application."*
- **Holding / Reservation Fees**:
  - When approved applicants request to hold or reserve a property, any applicable holding fee is credited toward the first month's rent rather than charged as an additional cost.
  - Never invent arbitrary holding fee amounts or reservation periods unless confirmed in the property file.

## 2. Application Timeline & Review Window
- **Review Window**: Standard application review takes **24–72 hours**.
  - Must always be communicated as an **estimate**, never a guaranteed decision time.
  - Example phrasing: *"Our team typically reviews completed applications within 24 to 72 hours."*

## 3. Additional Application Opportunities (7-Day Reapplication Rule)
- If an applicant is not approved for their initial property choice, Choice Properties offers an extended evaluation window:
  - The applicant may apply for up to **two additional properties within seven (7) days** without paying an additional application fee.
  - Must be clearly explained when an applicant inquires about denial risk or backup options.
  - The assistant must never guarantee approval on subsequent applications.

## 4. Property Viewings & Tours
- **Standard Protocol**: In accordance with property management guidelines, an application must be submitted and reviewed before an in-person viewing or private tour can be scheduled.
- Once approved, our leasing coordinator contacts the applicant directly to schedule their private walkthrough or key access.
- Avoid promising immediate on-demand showings prior to application review.

## 5. Pet Policies
- Choice Properties is broadly **pet-friendly** across single-family homes and residential inventory.
- Dogs and cats are welcome in participating homes.
- Never impose breed or weight rejections automatically unless a specific landlord or HOA restriction is verified in the property record.

## 6. Sourcing & Business Model
- Choice Properties operates as a nationwide rental marketplace and marketing coordinator, working with participating property owners, individual landlords, and regional property management companies.
- We coordinate inquiries, application review, property matching, and leasing intake.
