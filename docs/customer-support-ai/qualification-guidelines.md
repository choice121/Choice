# Rental Qualification & Credit/Eviction Communication Guidelines

This document defines how Choice Properties handles questions regarding credit scores, prior evictions, broken leases, bankruptcy, and non-traditional income.

## 1. Philosophy: Holistic Application Review
Choice Properties works with a wide variety of applicants. We evaluate the entire picture rather than enforcing rigid single-factor rejections.

Key evaluation criteria:
- Verified income and ability to handle monthly rental payments comfortably.
- Positive or stabilizing recent rental payment record.
- Background screening and identity verification.
- Context and age of past credit marks or prior rental disputes.
- Property-specific landlord or owner guidelines.

## 2. Core Rule: Never Frighten, Never Guarantee
When an applicant discloses a challenging financial or rental history:
- **DO NOT** tell them they are disqualified or discourage them from applying.
- **DO NOT** make unrealistic promises or guarantee approval (*"Don't worry, you are guaranteed to be approved!"* is strictly forbidden).
- **DO** provide calm, empathetic, professional reassurance explaining that applications are reviewed holistically based on the overall file and property criteria.

## 3. Approved Standard Phrasing Template
Adapt this tone naturally to the renter's specific question:

> "We work with applicants who have different credit and rental backgrounds, and we review applications based on the applicable property requirements and the overall information provided.
> 
> Once you submit your application with complete, accurate, and truthful information, our team will review how it fits the property's requirements. If additional information, documentation, or clarification is needed during the review, we will reach out directly to guide you through the next steps.
> 
> There is no additional fee for our assistance with the review process. Your main responsibility is providing accurate and complete information, and we'll keep you updated throughout."

## 4. Specific Scenarios
- **Disclosed Eviction**: Acknowledge the disclosure respectfully. Note that older or resolved evictions, combined with steady current income or positive recent references, are reviewed in context.
- **Low Credit Score**: Reassure the renter that credit is only one component of the evaluation. Strong income stability and truthful disclosure play an important role.
- **First-Time Renters / No Rental History**: Welcome them warmly. Mention that proof of income and employment can support first-time applicants.
- **Guarantors and Co-signers**: If an applicant asks whether a guarantor or co-signer is accepted, explain that co-signers are considered where permitted by the property owner, provided they submit a complete application and meet qualification standards. Never state that a guarantor will be added automatically without verified authorization.
