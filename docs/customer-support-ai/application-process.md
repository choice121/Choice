# Choice Properties — Rental Application Process & Workflow

This document outlines the standard customer journey from initial inquiry to lease signing.

## 1. The Standard Workflow
1. **Intake & Preference Discovery**: Identify the customer's target municipality, bedrooms, bathrooms, monthly budget, and move-in timeframe.
2. **Inventory Matching**: Match preferences against verified active inventory or curated Private Placement collections.
3. **Link Sharing**: Provide verified listing links or a single client Master Magic Link (`/listings.html#share=token`).
4. **Property Review & Selection**: The customer reviews options and identifies their top candidate property.
5. **Application Guidance**: Guide the applicant to the online application form (`/apply`).
6. **Application Fee Collection**: The application fee is $50 per adult screened. The form supports a primary applicant and one additional screened adult, displays the total before submission, and stores the household total on the application record. Staff must record payment for the exact displayed total; do not mark a partial payment as paid. Fees are subject to applicable law.
7. **Separate Adult Authorization**: Each adult being screened must personally authorize screening. The primary applicant's checkbox does not authorize screening for another adult. An additional adult receives a separate, one-use email link that expires after seven days; screening must not begin until that adult submits consent. An administrator can issue a replacement link if it expires or the email fails. Application approval is blocked until consent is confirmed.
8. **Underwriting & Review**: Application enters underwriting review after required payment and each screened adult's authorization are recorded (typically 24–72 hours).
9. **Document Follow-Up**: If proof of income (pay stubs, bank statements, offer letters) or photo ID is needed, staff contacts the applicant directly.
10. **Outcome Communication**: Staff communicates approval or decision. After an initial paid application is not approved, the 30-day courtesy may cover up to two additional participating properties for the same screened adults; newly screened adults are subject to the standard fee. Staff must verify eligibility and apply any fee waiver before collecting payment.
11. **Viewing & Property Reservation**: Approved applicant schedules a private viewing and submits the holding deposit (which credits to 1st month's rent).
12. **Lease Execution**: Digital lease agreement is delivered and signed.

## 2. Screening Consent and Legal Review
- A $50 charge per adult screened is the configured business rule, not a universal legal standard. Before operating in a jurisdiction, qualified counsel should confirm applicable fee caps, timing, disclosure, refund, and consumer-report authorization requirements.
- Consent copy and this workflow are technical controls, not legal advice. Counsel should review the screening disclosure and authorization, including any required standalone disclosure, written authorization, state/local notices, and adverse-action process.
- Never infer one adult's permission from another applicant's submission. Keep screening paused until each screened adult's own consent is recorded.

## 3. Process vs. Confirmed Action Discipline
- **Crucial Distinction**: The assistant must always distinguish between *explaining the workflow* and *confirming a status*.
- NEVER claim that:
  - An application has been submitted,
  - A fee has been received or verified,
  - An application has been approved, or
  - A home has been reserved,
  unless that status is explicitly confirmed in the customer's profile by staff.
- If status is unconfirmed, use *"Not confirmed"* internally, and invite the applicant to provide confirmation or check in.

## 3. Unavailable & Social Media Listing Scenarios
- When a customer inquires about a home from TikTok, Instagram, or an older link that is no longer active:
  - Thank them for sharing the property.
  - Transparently explain that the specific home is no longer available or currently under application review.
  - Pivot warmly to finding an active home that matches their criteria in that area.
  - Check their profile: if target beds, baths, and budget are known, recommend available properties immediately; if unknown, request only the missing criteria.
