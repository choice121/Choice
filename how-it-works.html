For Renters

A clear path from search to next steps.

Review each listing's details and follow its application instructions.

How It Works

01
Browse Listings
Compare current listings, location, rent, and property details.
02
Submit an Application
Apply online for the home you choose. The fee is $50 per adult being screened, and the total is shown before submission.
03
Management Review
The team responsible for the listing reviews completed applications and shares updates according to that listing's process.
04
Lease and Move-In
If approved, follow the listing contact's instructions for the next steps, which may include arranging a viewing and coordinating lease details.

Before You Apply

  • The application review process and contact details are provided with the listing
  • Use the available application status or contact channel for updates
  • Clear listing details and a $50 fee per adult being screened
  • Lease coordination and electronic signing
