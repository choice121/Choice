Authorize your own screening

You were listed as an additional adult applicant or guarantor on a Choice Properties rental application. This step is separate from the primary applicant's application and consent.

Authorization

By checking the box below and submitting, you authorize Choice Properties and its designated verification providers to verify information you provide, including employment, income, rental history, and personal references, and to request consumer reports where applicable. If a decision is based on a consumer report, you will receive the notices and information required by law.

The screening application fee is $50 per adult screened. The primary applicant was shown the total fee for the adults included in the application. Your screening will not begin until your authorization is recorded.

This secure, one-time link expires after seven days. If you did not expect this request, do not submit it. Contact support@choiceproperties.com for help.
