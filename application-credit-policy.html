Application Credit Policy

How credits are issued after a denial, how to use them, and what the limits are.

Effective Date: April 22, 2026  ·  Version 2.0  ·  Changelog

Home › Policies › Application Credit Policy

What Are Application Credits?

Application credits provide complete financial clarity and protection for Choice Properties prospective tenants. When you submit and pay the application fee for a home, your application credit is valid for 30 days and is applied directly toward your move-in costs upon lease signing. Furthermore, the application credit covers the total applicants who applied on the household application (primary applicant plus all screened co-applicants and guarantors).

If your application is not approved for a specific property, your paid application fee converts into a 30-day household credit that can be applied toward reapplying for any other available Choice Properties home, or credited towards move-in costs when you lease.

How Credits Are Applied Towards Move-In Costs

  • Full Household Coverage: The application credit covers the total applicants that applied. For instance, if a household with 3 applicants paid $150 ($50 × 3), the full $150 is credited towards your move-in balance.
  • Direct Rent / Deposit Deduction: When you execute a lease for a Choice Properties listing, the entire amount paid in application fees is credited directly on your move-in accounting statement toward your first month's rent or security deposit.
  • 30-Day Reapplication Courtesy: If an application is not approved for a particular property, your credit remains active for 30 days, allowing your household to apply for another available home without paying duplicate screening fees.

Credit Validity

  • Application credits remain valid for 30 days from the date of fee confirmation
  • Covers the total applicants and guarantors included on the household application
  • Credits are credited directly against move-in costs upon lease execution
  • Credits have no cash redemption value and cannot be redeemed for an unearned cash refund

How to Use Credits

When your lease is prepared, our accounting system automatically credits 100% of your paid application fees against your move-in costs:

  • The total application fees paid for all household members are listed as a credit on your lease financial summary
  • Your total move-in balance is reduced dollar-for-dollar by the application credit amount
  • If reapplying for a different property within 30 days of a non-approval, management verifies your eligibility and applies your household credit

Conditions for Credit Eligibility

Credits are only issued when:

  • The application was fully completed (all required steps)
  • The application fee was paid
  • The application was submitted and received by the system
  • The application was formally denied (Not Selected) by the landlord or platform

Credits are NOT issued for:

  • Applications withdrawn before payment was completed
  • Applications withdrawn after active review has begun (screening costs will have been incurred)
  • Incomplete or abandoned applications
  • Applications that were not paid

Exception — pre-review withdrawal after payment: If you withdraw your application after completing payment but before active screening has started, you will automatically receive credits as if the application had been denied. This ensures you are not penalized for a timely change of mind before any screening costs are incurred.

Abuse Prevention

The credit system is intended to protect genuine applicants from financial loss when denied. Misuse of the system — including submitting applications with no genuine intent in order to accumulate credits — violates the Terms of Service and may result in account suspension and forfeiture of all credits.

Credits are:

  • Non-transferable (tied to your email only)
  • Not combinable (one credit covers one application fee, regardless of fee amount)
  • Not stackable beyond 2 active credits at any time

Disputes

If you believe credits were not issued after a denial, contact Choice Properties at 707-706-3137 (Text Only) with your Application ID.
