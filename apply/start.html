How the Application Process Works

01

Complete Your Application

Estimated time: 15–20 minutes across 6 organized sections. Your answers save automatically in your browser while you complete the form.

02

Transparent Household Fee

$50.00 for each adult applicant, co-applicant, guarantor, or co-signer — subject to state fee limits where applicable.

03

Choose Your Preferred Payment Method

Select your 1st, 2nd, and 3rd choice payment methods on the application (Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Apple Pay, Chime, Card, or Other). Within 24 hours after submitting, our team will contact you to arrange payment.

04

Review & Resident Portal

Underwriting begins once fee payment is confirmed (typically 24–48 hours). Track updates and upload additional files anytime in your Tenant Portal.

Fee & Household Rules

  • $50.00 Per Adult: $50 application fee per adult applicant, co-applicant, guarantor, or co-signer.
  • Method A or Method B: Enter household members' details yourself or send them a private self-completion invitation link (never creates a duplicate application).
  • No Card Required on Form: Choose your preferred payment method(s) in Step 5 — no card or bank details are collected on the application form.
  • 30-Day Credit Protection: If an application is not approved after payment, fee credits remain valid for 30 days toward another Choice Properties home (Credit Policy).

What Helpful Details to Have Ready

  • Identity Verification: Last 4 digits of SSN, or Alternative Government ID / ITIN / Passport option.
  • 24 Months of Residency: Current address and landlord contact (plus prior address if less than 24 months at your current home).
  • Income & Contacts: Employer address, start date, supervisor contact, gross monthly income, two references, and an emergency contact.
  • Non-Blocking Uploads: Photos or PDFs of your ID (front/back) and income proof can be attached on Step 6 or uploaded later in your portal — skipping uploads never blocks submission.

How Your Information Is Protected

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. We collect only the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number (never full 9-digit SSNs) or an accepted alternative government ID, and uploaded documents are stored in private access-controlled buckets (never cached as unencrypted browser drafts).
