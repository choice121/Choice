Simple Process

From browse to keys, in three clear steps.

Apply, get reviewed, sign, move in. Every step is transparent — and you always know where you stand.

  1. 01

    Find your home

    Browse rental listings across our active markets. Filter by bedrooms, price, property type, and more to find your perfect fit.

  2. 02

    Apply & activate review

    Submit your application and coordinate the $50-per-screened-adult fee total shown on the form. Management reviews completed applications and shares status updates.

  3. 03

    Reserve, sign & move in

    If selected, reserve your unit with a holding fee (credited toward move-in), sign your lease digitally, and coordinate move-in. Welcome home.
