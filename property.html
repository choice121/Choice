The Process — Step by Step
1
Apply Online
Select this property and submit your details. Nothing is charged at initial submission — your application is placed in queue once the application fee is paid.
Free to submit
2
Pay the Application Fee
The application fee activates your screening review. Completing payment promptly secures your order in the applicant review line.
$50 — non-refundable once processed
3
Active Screening (24–72 Hours)
Your background, employment, and rental history are processed. Decisions are delivered within 24 to 72 hours. Prompt applicants are prioritized.
4
Approval & Selection
Approved applicants receive immediate email and portal notifications. Units are reserved on a first-completion basis.
5
Pay the Holding Deposit
Reserves the property and takes it off the market for 7–14 days while the lease is prepared. This converts directly into your move-in security deposit.
100% credited toward your move-in costs
6
Sign Your Lease
Review and securely sign your standardized lease agreement online with legally-binding digital signatures.
7
Move In & Key Handover
Coordinate key collection, move-in walkthrough inspection, and welcome package with the landlord.
